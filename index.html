About ESAIL

Helping societies prepare for an AI-powered future.

ESAIL brings together researchers, educators, civic organisations, policymakers, and citizens to improve understanding of artificial intelligence and its social impact.

We believe that AI literacy is not only a technical skill, but a public responsibility. By promoting critical thinking, ethical awareness, and practical knowledge, we support a Europe where people can participate in informed digital decisions.

Our mission

Promoting understanding and participation at every level.

01

Education

Supporting schools, universities, and lifelong learners with clear, accessible AI education.

02

Ethics

Encouraging responsible and transparent use of AI in public and private life.

03

Participation

Empowering citizens and communities to engage with digital policy and innovation.

Focus areas

Where we work to strengthen AI literacy.

Public understanding

Clear explanations of how AI works, what it can and cannot do, and how it affects daily life.

Policy & governance

Helping citizens, institutions, and decision-makers understand AI regulation and accountability.

Skills & training

Building practical digital and AI competencies across education, work, and community settings.

Inclusive access

Ensuring AI literacy is accessible, multilingual, and relevant to diverse communities.
